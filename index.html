PROJECT DETAILS

3.1 Attribution
▼
Describe and document how you ensure that the project activities are attributable to the carbon credit program. Include a statement that clearly identifies that credit for the project is not already accounted for or claimed elsewhere.
Include confirmation that project credit is not already accounted for or claimed in any other carbon credit program or climate policy mechanism.
Describe the mechanisms in place to ensure that the scope 1 units produced are clearly identified and distinguished from any similar units not generated as part of the project.
Specify the geographic locations where the project activities occur. This may include multiple regions, countries, or sub-national units. The geographic boundaries must be clearly delineated in the map(s) provided in Section 2.6.
Ensure that geographic boundaries align with the map(s) provided in Section 2.6 (Project Area and Location).
Specify the time period during which the project generates scope 1 units. This includes the project start date, issuance periods, and, if applicable, the project end date.
Provide evidence that the project activities have not been and will not be duplicated in any other carbon credit program, climate policy mechanism, or regulatory scheme. This includes national or international emissions trading systems.
Confirm that the project complies with all applicable local, regional, and national laws and regulations. The project must not rely on non-compliance for its viability.
Upload any supporting documents that provide evidence of attribution (e.g., legal agreements, unique identification systems, geographic maps, regulatory compliance certificates).
Supported formats: PDF, DOC, DOCX, JPG, PNG, XLSX
